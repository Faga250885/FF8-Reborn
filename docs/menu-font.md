# Localized menu glyphs

The extra bitmap glyphs in `src/menu_glyphs.inc` are a subset of Shinonome 12-dot by Yasuyuki Furukawa and the Electronic Font Open Laboratory. The source fonts are public domain. [Source and license](https://github.com/code4fukui/shinonome-font/blob/053b21e0a11ef5799c1ea3cefc374763add80617/LICENSE)

Japanese glyphs come from `12/kanjic/font_src.bit` and Latin accents from `12/latin1/font_src.bit`. The renderer uses the embedded glyphs on Windows and Steam Deck.
